Step-by-step: how to add ads

Step 1: Build and publish your site.
It should be live on a real domain, with original content (pages, navigation, a privacy policy page, an about/contact page). Google reviews the quality of the site before approving it.

Step 2: Create an AdSense account.
Go to https://www.google.com/adsense/start/, click “Get Started” and sign in with your Google account, then enter your site URL.
